Return to Index

On all screens you can tap the < index link in the top left corner. In the app you can also tap with three fingers simultaneously. Completing a journey will also return you to the Index automatically.

Highlighted Interactive areas

The Principle Mirror iPhone app has a feature that you can turn on in your iOS settings app to show you interactive areas. The web version does not, however you can follow the click guide below to see each journey in full.

Prototype click guide

Journey 01 - Marketplace

Step Action
01 tap Download or swipe up on instagram story
02 tap Get
03 tap Open
04 tap Done
05 tap Marketplace on tab bar navigation
06 tap Search icon (top right)
07 tap Search bar
08 tap Product 3 (E-bike bundle)
09 tap Buy now
10 tap Checkout
11 tap Log in
12 tap Log in (after credentials are populated)
13 tap Confirm
14 wait then tap or drag iOS notification
15 tap the Close icon or wait to go back to Index

Journey 02 - Rewards

Step Action
01 tap Reward CVM (first item in carousel)
02 wait or tap Next
03 wait or tap Next
04 tap Discover rewards
05 scroll down to Catagories and tap Near me
06 play around then tap the Close icon (top right)
07 scroll back to top and tap the Spin to Win CVM
08 tap or spin the Wheel
09 tap Take the 1000 V-Points
10 tap Continue with my choice
11 tap Log in (after details are populated)
12 tap Done or wait to go back to Index

Journey 03 - Home Hub

Step Action
01 tap Devices detected CVM (first item in carousel)
02 wait or tap Next
03 wait or tap Next
04 tap Discover Home Hub
05 select / deselect devices and tap Add devices
06 wait then tap or drag iOS notification
07 tap Camera toggle (turn camera on)
08 tap Going out
09 tap Close icon (top right of screen)
10 tap Continue with my choice
11 wait to go back to Index

Journey 04 - Support

Step Action
01 tap or drag iOS notification
02 tap Start health check
03 tap Yes
04 tap Switch now
05 tap Checkbox (to use V-points)
06 tap Confirm
07 tap Gaming hub CVM (first item in carousel)
08 tap Ben's Phone Checkbox
09 wait then tap or drag iOS notification
10 wait to go back to Index

Journey 05 - Play around

This has no linear path, you can freely browse the main sections.

Step Action
01 General - tap the Navigation bar items
02 General - tap TOBi
03 Dashboard - tap Account switcher (top right)
04 Dashboard - swipe the Latest for you carousel
05 Dashboard - tap the Next bill tile
06 Dashboard - tap the other Services tiles
07 Dashboard - swipe the My products carousel
08 Marketplace - tap the Search icon (top right)
09 Marketplace - swipe the Popular this week carousel
10 Rewards - swipe the Your rewards carousel
11 Home Hub - long press anywhere on the main content to cycle though three different examples
12 Home Hub - tap V-Home Safety (in two examples with Smart Tech)
